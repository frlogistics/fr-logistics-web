EcoPack+: Miami's Greenest Package Pickup Service Is Here

Operations Manager, FR-Logistics Miami

Today, we're officially launching EcoPack+ — a brand new service from FR-Logistics designed for the Miami community. If you live in Doral, Hialeah, Sweetwater, or anywhere nearby, this one's for you.

The idea is simple: use our warehouse address when you shop online. We receive everything, inspect it, repack it in our signature eco-friendly green bag, and you come pick it up whenever it's convenient. No missed deliveries. No porch pirates. And most importantly — zero packaging waste left behind.

🌿 EcoPack+ is free to join. Registration takes 2 minutes and our team will contact you within 24 hours with your personal delivery address.

Why We Built EcoPack+

After more than 20 years in logistics, I've seen the amount of cardboard, bubble wrap, plastic, and foam that gets thrown away every single day — just from e-commerce deliveries alone. Miami residents receive thousands of packages daily, and most of that packaging ends up in the trash.

At the same time, I kept hearing the same frustration from people around Doral: missed deliveries, stolen packages, and the inconvenience of having to be home at the right time. We realized we could solve both problems at once.

EcoPack+ uses FR-Logistics' existing warehouse infrastructure — the same facility we use for Amazon FBA prep and Shopify fulfillment — and opens it up to the local community. You get the security and convenience of a professional receiving service, and we handle all the recycling. Everyone wins.

The "Eco" Part — What Actually Happens to Your Packaging

This is the part I'm most proud of. When you leave your boxes, bubble wrap, and plastic bags at our facility, they don't go to a landfill. We've partnered with local Miami recycling centers to ensure every material gets properly processed and given a second life.

Think about how much packaging waste you generate in a single month of online shopping. Now multiply that across thousands of households in Doral and surrounding cities. That's a lot of cardboard. EcoPack+ consolidates that waste at the warehouse level, where it can be recycled efficiently.

Who Is EcoPack+ For?

EcoPack+ is designed for anyone who lives or works near the Doral area and shops online regularly. That said, a few groups will find it especially useful:

  • Busy professionals who can't be home for deliveries during work hours
  • Apartment residents without secure package receiving areas
  • Families who shop frequently from multiple online retailers
  • Eco-conscious consumers who want to reduce their packaging waste footprint
  • Small business owners who receive supplier shipments and want a professional receiving service

📍 Our warehouse is located at 10893 NW 17th St Unit 121, Doral FL 33172 — minutes from Miami International Airport and easily accessible from Hialeah, Sweetwater, Medley, Tamiami, and Fontainebleau.

Meet LIAM — The Face of EcoPack+

Every great service deserves a great mascot. Meet LIAM — which stands for Logistics Intelligence Assistant Manager. Our friendly green bag character represents everything EcoPack+ stands for: sustainability, community, and making logistics accessible and fun for everyone.

You'll see LIAM on our eco bags, our social media, and throughout the EcoPack+ experience. He's our reminder that doing good for the environment doesn't have to be complicated — sometimes it's as simple as dropping off a cardboard box.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EcoPack+ really free?

Yes — registration is completely free. Once you're registered, our team will discuss service pricing based on your usage. Contact us at info@fr-logistics.net for details.

What stores can I use?

Any online store that ships to a US address — Amazon, Walmart, Target, Shein, AliExpress, eBay, Etsy, and more. If it ships to Miami, we can receive it.

How do I know when my package arrives?

Our team will notify you by SMS or email as soon as your package is received and ready for pickup.

What are the pickup hours?

Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We're located at 10893 NW 17th St Unit 121, Doral FL 33172.

Is my package secure?

Absolutely. All packages are received with your pre-authorization and stored securely in our professional warehouse facility — the same one we use for Amazon FBA prep operations.
