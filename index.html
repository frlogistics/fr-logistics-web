Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything you want to know before you commit. No sales pitch.

Do you have minimum order volumes or contracts?
No. We don't require minimum monthly volume, and all service is month-to-month. If it isn't working for you, you leave. Your first month of storage is on us.
How fast is the onboarding process?
Most clients are live in 3 to 5 business days. The process: discovery call, signed Operations Agreement, inventory receiving, platform integration, first orders shipping. You get a dedicated onboarding checklist in both English and Spanish.
Do you accept shipments from China, LATAM, or other international suppliers?
Yes. We receive FCL and LCL containers from PortMiami and Port Everglades, air cargo from MIA, and direct drop-offs from freight forwarders. Full container unloading, palletization, inspection, labeling, and storage — with photo documentation on arrival.
What's the cutoff for same-day shipping?
Orders received by 2:00 PM ET, Monday-Friday, ship the same day. Orders after cutoff ship the next business day. FBA prep shipments have a 24-48 hour turnaround depending on volume.
What platforms and carriers do you integrate with?
Platforms: Amazon (SPN), Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Walmart DSV, eBay, TikTok Shop. Carriers: USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, regional LTL/FTL. Systems: ShipStation, Linnworks, SkuVault, Easyship. You can also bring your own carrier account — we integrate via API or accept pre-generated labels.
How do you handle Amazon returns (RMA)?
We receive Amazon customer returns at our Doral facility, inspect each unit with photo documentation, and categorize as restock, repack, resell, or dispose based on condition. Full condition report per unit. Fit-for-resale units are relabeled and returned to FBA inventory or your DTC pool.
Do you operate in Spanish?
Sí, 100%. Our entire operation — onboarding, contracts, WhatsApp support, invoicing, reporting — is available in Spanish and English. We serve LATAM sellers from Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Peru, Chile, and beyond.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. Our rate card is published on this site — something most 3PLs hide behind "Request a Quote." You pay only for: receiving (per carton), storage (per pallet/day), and the value-added services you use. No WMS fees, no monthly minimums.
