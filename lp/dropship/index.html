Which model fits you?

We serve LATAM sellers under two distinct operational models. Your setup defines how we work together.

Model 1

Buy in US, sell in LATAM

Your end customer orders on Mercado Libre or your store. You buy the product on Amazon US, Walmart, or Home Depot, ship it to our warehouse, and we handle everything else.

  • We receive packages from Amazon, Walmart, Home Depot, Target, Best Buy, Costco and more
  • Remove all retailer branding and packing slips
  • Repack with neutral materials (no third-party identifiers)
  • Process the label from your platform or your carrier account
  • Photo confirmation of every outbound package
Retail arbitrage · Online arbitrage · Reseller
Model 2

Inventory in Miami, sales in LATAM

You have your own stock stored at our Doral warehouse. When an order comes in via Mercado Libre, Shopify, or TikTok Shop, we pick, pack, and hand it to the carrier you define.

  • Pallet or shelf storage · first month free
  • Pick & pack from your own inventory
  • Optional branded packaging (your brand, inserts, note)
  • Integration with ShipStation, Shopify, Amazon, eBay
  • Real-time inventory in your client portal
Brand owner · Private label · DTC LATAM

Frequently asked questions

Is this a job offer or a service I pay for?

It's a paid B2B service. FR-Logistics is a 3PL warehouse in Doral, Miami. Companies and sellers hire us and pay us from $6 per package processed to store, process, and ship their products. We do not offer employment, we do not pay people to do dropshipping, and we do not sell training. If you're looking for a job or paid side hustle, this is not the right page.

Do you handle international shipping Miami → LATAM?

Not directly. We process your full order (receiving, repack, label, hand-off to carrier), but international shipping is handled with your carrier of choice: DHL, FedEx, UPS, Aeropost, Mercado Envíos, or the courier you have contracted. We work with your account or generate the label from your platform.

How do you connect with Mercado Libre and my other channels?

We integrate with your order management platform — ShipStation, Linnworks, SkuVault, Shopify Apps. If you sell on Mercado Libre, Amazon MX/BR, Shopify, and TikTok Shop simultaneously, all those orders can be centralized in ShipStation and we process them from there. We do not connect directly to Mercado Libre's API — always through your OMS.

How much do you charge for dropship?

Typical dropship rate goes from $6 per order processed, depending on monthly volume and additional services (kitting, branded packaging, extra photos). We quote in a discovery call after understanding your volume and specific operations. No monthly fees, no minimums.

What about taxes and customs?

The package leaves US as a normal export. Customs management at destination is handled by your international carrier (DHL, FedEx, etc.) or the broker you designate. We include the commercial invoice and packing slip with the information you provide us. We do not act as a customs broker.

How fast does my package leave Miami after you receive it?

If your inbound package or pick & pack order arrives before 2 PM ET, we process it and hand it to the carrier the same day. After that, it ships the next business day. Monday through Friday.

How do I know you correctly removed the retailer branding?

Every outbound order includes before/after photo confirmation of the repack. Photos are documented in your client portal. Zero Amazon, Walmart or any retailer packing slips end up in your end customer's hands.

Do you work with Mercado Envíos Full / Flex?

Yes. If your carrier of choice is Mercado Envíos (Flex or Full), we process the order with the label Mercado Libre generates for you and hand it to the pickup point or the courier ML designates. The ML API integration is on your side; we operate the physical fulfillment.
