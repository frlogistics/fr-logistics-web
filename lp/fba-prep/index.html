Quick answers before you reach out

Do you require monthly minimums or contracts?

No. We don't require any minimum monthly volume. All service is month-to-month. If it isn't working for you, you leave. Your first month of storage is on us.

How fast can I get started?

Most clients are live within 3 to 5 business days. The process: discovery call, signed Operations Agreement, inventory receiving, platform integration, first orders shipping.

Do you accept shipments from China, LATAM or international suppliers?

Yes. We receive FCL and LCL containers from PortMiami and Port Everglades, air cargo from MIA, and direct drop-offs from freight forwarders. Full unloading, inspection, labeling, and storage with photo documentation.

What about restricted or regulated products?

We do not handle hazmat (UN Class 1-9), cannabis, vape, or flavored pods. Cigars, supplements, medical devices and CBD federally legal may be handled through our Traceability service after compliance review with Jose Fuentes.
