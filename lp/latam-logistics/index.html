LATAM logistics — frequently asked

Is this a job offer or a paid service?

It's a paid B2B service. FR-Logistics is a 3PL warehouse in Doral, Miami. LATAM exporters and brands hire us and pay us per-unit and per-carton rates to receive, inspect, prep, and inbound their inventory to Amazon USA/Canada. We do not offer employment, we do not pay people to source or do dropshipping, and we do not sell training. If you're looking for a job or side hustle, this is not the right page.

Do you handle customs clearance from LATAM?

No. Customs clearance is handled by your freight forwarder or customs broker. We receive shipments that have already cleared US Customs. We coordinate directly with your forwarder on appointment, documentation, and delivery.

What kind of inbound do you accept?

Everything. FCL containers direct from PortMiami or Port Everglades. LCL consolidations. Air cargo from MIA. Direct truck deliveries from freight forwarders. We coordinate appointment scheduling with your provider.

Which LATAM countries do you serve?

Currently serving sellers from Mexico, Colombia, Peru, Chile, Argentina, Brazil, Ecuador, Venezuela, and Panama. If you're elsewhere in LATAM, talk to us — we can probably make it work.

How fast does inventory move from arrival to Amazon FC?

Standard turnaround is 3-5 business days from receipt to Amazon inbound. Rush 24-48h available on coordination. Multiple prep stations run in parallel — we don't bottleneck on volume.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. 100% bilingual team. WhatsApp, email, phone, video calls — all in Spanish if you prefer. Our operations team handles communications in your timezone.

Do you require contracts or monthly minimums?

No contracts. No monthly minimums. Month-to-month service. Pay only for receiving, storage, and prep you actually use. No hidden fees, no platform fees.

Ready to ship from LATAM to Amazon USA?

Tell us your country of origin and monthly volume. We come back within 24 hours with a custom plan — including projected costs and onboarding timeline.
