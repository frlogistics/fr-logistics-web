Wholesale-specific questions

Can the brand ship directly to your warehouse?

Yes. We accept brand-direct shipments, distributor LTL, FCL containers, and air cargo. Just share the ASN and PO ahead of arrival. We notify you on receipt with photo documentation.

How fast is FBA prep at high volume?

Standard turnaround is 3-5 business days from receipt to inbound. Rush (24-48h) available on coordination. We don't bottleneck — multiple prep stations run in parallel.

What about restricted or regulated categories?

We handle most categories. Not allowed: hazmat (UN Class 1-9), cannabis, vape, flavored pods. Cigars, supplements, medical devices, and CBD-federally-legal go through our Traceability service with compliance validation.

What happens if Amazon rejects a shipment?

If the rejection is due to our prep error, we re-prep and re-ship at our cost. If it's due to Amazon-side issues, we coordinate the return, document everything, and re-inbound. Either way, you stay informed.

Do you require contracts or monthly minimums?

No contracts. No monthly minimums. Month-to-month. You pay only for receiving, storage, and value-added services you actually use. No hidden fees, no platform fees, no surprise charges.

Ready to scale your FBA operation?

Tell us your monthly volume and brand mix. We'll come back within 24 hours with a custom plan — including projected costs and onboarding timeline.
