Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle customs clearance?
We work with a network of trusted licensed customs brokers in Miami and can refer you. We don't act as customs broker ourselves, but we coordinate the handoff so your shipment moves smoothly.
What countries do you receive from?
Most of our LATAM clients ship from Colombia, Mexico, Argentina, Peru, Chile, and Brazil. We also handle imports from China and Vietnam for Amazon sellers.
Can you forward directly to Amazon FBA?
Yes. We're Amazon SPN-certified. We do the FNSKU labeling, polybagging, kitting and carton prep — then ship directly to Amazon's fulfillment centers per your shipment plan.
How is pricing structured?
Receiving is per carton, storage is per pallet/month, FBA prep is per unit. We send you a custom rate card after a 15-min call to understand your volume.
